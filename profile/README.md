# Stuff of Awe

**Good things, gathered.**

A collection of awesome lists for people who find provers, solvers, programming languages and unlikely computational ideas rather exciting.

Some things come from our own workshop; many come from elsewhere. The point is to collect useful, surprising work, give its makers proper credit, and help you find the next thing worth exploring.

## Open a drawer

- [awesome-nickel](https://github.com/stuff-of-awe/awesome-nickel) — Nickel and configuration.
- [awesome-idris2](https://github.com/stuff-of-awe/awesome-idris2) — Idris 2 and dependent types.
- [awesome-haskell](https://github.com/stuff-of-awe/awesome-haskell) — Haskell and functional programming.
- [awesome-zig](https://github.com/stuff-of-awe/awesome-zig) — Zig and systems programming.
- [awesome-coq](https://github.com/stuff-of-awe/awesome-coq) — Coq/Rocq and proof.

[Browse all collections](https://github.com/orgs/stuff-of-awe/repositories)

Further drawers are planned for provers and solvers, and later JTV and AffineScript. We will link them here when they exist.

## Found something worth keeping?

Suggest it in the relevant list with a working link and a sentence explaining why it belongs. Contributions from outside our own projects are welcome. Where a collection is a fork, retain its upstream attribution and follow that repository’s contribution guidance.

[Contribution guidance](https://github.com/stuff-of-awe/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)
