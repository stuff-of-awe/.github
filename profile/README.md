# Stuff of Awe

**Good things, gathered. Knowledge worth putting to work.**

We are building a community-maintained home for excellent resources in mathematics, logic, theorem proving, constraint solving and the programming languages that support them.

Our mission is to help people **find a tool, understand it, try it and build on it**: from a first proof to research libraries, benchmarks and practical verification workflows. Our vision is a connected collection that makes mathematical software easier to discover and use, including the smaller projects that deserve more attention.

Some resources come from our own workshop; many come from elsewhere. Inclusion depends on usefulness and quality, with credit to the people who made them. You do not need to use our projects to benefit or contribute.

## A home for provers and solvers

[ECHIDNA](https://github.com/hyperpolymath/echidna) provides our starting coverage inventory: proof assistants, automated theorem provers, SMT and SAT solvers, model checkers, verification tools and optimisation systems. **It is a starting point, not the limit of our scope.**

The [coverage ledger](https://github.com/stuff-of-awe/.github/blob/main/docs/collections/CATALOGUE.adoc) accounts for all 141 declared entries in the reviewed ECHIDNA snapshot, distinguishes internal dispatch categories from tool ecosystems, and marks existing collections and remaining gaps. This is an inventory, not a claim that all integrations or collections are complete.

We want practical, locally maintained collections: clear descriptions, canonical documentation, learning paths, libraries, examples and honest limitations. Where good lists already exist, we will credit and work with them. Where there is a gap, we will develop a useful collection here.

Lean, Isabelle, Z3, cvc5 and F* are among the next priorities. F* is distinct from F#. Collections for JTV, AffineScript and other projects can follow as public resources become available.
