= Provers, solvers and mathematical tools: coverage ledger
:toc:

Reviewed 2026-11-01.

== Purpose and evidence

Stuff of Awe is building maintained, practical collections for the mathematics,
logic and formal-methods communities. ECHIDNA supplies an initial coverage floor,
not a boundary: resources need not integrate with ECHIDNA or belong to our estate.

This ledger accounts for all *141 declared ProverKind variants* in
https://github.com/hyperpolymath/echidna/blob/916b7690a30bc3a42fd225d1c32140f41f20a580/crates/echidna-core/src/prover_kind.rs[ECHIDNA at commit 916b7690].
There are 100 entries outside the contiguous 41-entry internal dispatcher family.
These are declaration counts, not counts of independent products, tested adapters,
working installations or completed collections. Aliases and related ecosystems
share a collection where appropriate.

* *Existing*: a local repository exists; its completeness and link health still require review.
* *Starter*: an initial curated collection; ongoing expansion and review remain.
* *Planned*: a proposed repository name, deliberately not a link to a nonexistent repository.
* *Internal*: an ECHIDNA/estate dispatch category to document within a shared type-systems collection.

The upstream awesome-list survey is preliminary, not exhaustive. A search that
finds no exact name does not establish that no useful collection exists.

== Upstream discovery and attribution

Initial candidates found on 2026-11-01:

* https://github.com/freeman42x/awesome-agda-programming[awesome-agda-programming]
  and https://github.com/biocini/awesome-agda[awesome-agda]: compare scope and
  provenance with the existing local Agda collection before importing changes.
* https://github.com/fsprojects/awesome-fsharp[fsprojects/awesome-fsharp]:
  an upstream F# collection to compare with our existing local collection.
* https://github.com/pintjuk/awesome-theorem-proving[awesome-theorem-proving]:
  a cross-ecosystem discovery source.

Discovery does not imply endorsement, permission to copy, current maintenance,
or an established upstream relationship. Preserve authorship, licences and
history when importing material. Contribute fixes upstream where appropriate.
For Vampire, no dedicated awesome-vampire result was found in the initial search;
the starter uses original descriptions and canonical project resources.

== Next collection releases

. Review and strengthen the existing Agda, Coq/Rocq, Idris2 and provable-code lists.
. Expand the Vampire starter with worked proof workflows and community contributions.
. Research upstream lists and prepare Lean, Isabelle, Z3, cvc5 and F* collections.
. Continue through the complete ledger by family, including less prominent tools.
. Add mathematical software beyond ECHIDNA; JTV and AffineScript can have their
  own collections once useful public resources exist.

Each release needs useful initial content, provenance, a curator or explicit
call for one, contribution guidance and a dated review. A repository name alone
does not count as maintained coverage. See link:MAINTENANCE.adoc[the maintenance policy].
